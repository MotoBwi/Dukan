Chunne ke saath ye bhi bata dena:
  • Shop ka naam, address, phone (aur GSTIN ya UPI ID chahiye to wo bhi) — invoice par chhapega.
  • Invoice mein customer ka purana baaki (previous balance) dikhana hai ya sirf is bill ka?
  • Amount in words chahiye? Bhasha English ho ya Hindi?
  • Ek bill ki saari items ek hi invoice mein aayengi. Invoice number = Bill #, isi se search karke khulega.
